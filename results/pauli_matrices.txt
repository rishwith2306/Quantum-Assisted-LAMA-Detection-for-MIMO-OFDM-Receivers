Pauli matrices used in the proposed detector

I = [1 0; 0 1]
X = [0 1; 1 0]  -> mixer gate in QAOA
Y = [0 -i; i 0] -> not required for BPSK Ising cost, useful in general quantum operations
Z = [1 0; 0 -1] -> maps BPSK symbol +1/-1 to qubit eigenvalues

Cost Hamiltonian for reduced uncertain set U:
H_C = sum_i h_i Z_i + sum_{i<j} J_ij Z_i Z_j

Mixer Hamiltonian:
H_M = sum_i X_i
